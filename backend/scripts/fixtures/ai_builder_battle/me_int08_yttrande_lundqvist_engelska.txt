Dear Planning Department,

I am the owner of the property Sundsvall Tallmon 3:9 at Tallmovägen 9. I have lived and worked in Edinburgh since 2019, so I hope you will accept my comments in English. My brother looks after the house and forwarded the consultation letter to me.

My property lies downstream of the planned development, next to Björkbäcken. During the cloudburst in August 2023 the stream overflowed and water reached the ground floor of our house; the insurance claim was about 180 000 kronor. The plan will replace farmland with roofs, streets and car parks, and the stormwater will be led to Björkbäcken. The planning description says the stormwater will be delayed in open ponds, but it does not show how much more water will reach the stream during a heavy rain, or what happens when the ponds are full.

I am also concerned that the culvert under Tallmovägen is too small. It was blocked by debris twice last year, according to my neighbours, and I cannot see that the stormwater investigation has looked at it.

I therefore request:

- that the municipality carries out a cloudburst analysis that shows the flow in Björkbäcken downstream of the plan area, including at Sundsvall Tallmon 3:9, before the plan is sent out for review,
- that the capacity of the culvert under Tallmovägen is investigated and, if needed, increased before the new area is built,
- that all correspondence about the plan is also sent to me by e-mail, since I do not receive post at the property.

Thank you for considering my comments. I would be grateful for a confirmation that they have been received.

Kind regards,

Erik Lundqvist
Owner of Sundsvall Tallmon 3:9
