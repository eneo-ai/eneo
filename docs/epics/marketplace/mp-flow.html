How sharing works, from one municipality to another

Sundsvall builds a Flow. Örebro installs a copy. The same path works for a Skill, an Assistant, an App or an MCP definition.

1. Export from EneoOne .eneopkg file. Prompts and settings inside; knowledge and credentials left out and listed.
2. Publish on the HubUpload, describe what it does and needs, add screenshots, set a version and changelog.
3. Curator reviewsSees every prompt, Skill, script and step. Approves or rejects with a reason. Never their own.
4. Version 1.0 publishedImmutable. Visible to all members or to one organisation. Listed with prerequisites.
5. Örebro browses in EneoReads the listing and the prerequisites. Picks a target: personal space, shared space or organisation.
6. Plan, full content, confirmMaps models and sources, sees missing permissions, reads everything that will be created. Confirms.
7. A local copy with a receiptNew draft in Örebro's Eneo. Receipt: listing, version, digest, who. Audit on both sides.

Later: version 1.1 appears as a notice on the copy. Installing it creates a new copy. A yanked version shows a warning and the copy keeps working.
